# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

make.fun bounty entry: a 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

The dark lord has ruled the Ashen Coast for three hundred years, cursed harvests, drowned fleets, and signed the Tithe of Teeth in blood. Tonight, facing the latest torch-bearing hero, his greatest enemy is the staircase: twenty-one steps, counted twice. The tiredness lives in the counting, the pauses, and the small deflections, never announced outright. The sting lands in the last line: after the bell he naps, and he does not wake for prophecies.

Run `main.ts` with Node 22; it writes the monologue to `(MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR ?? "/output")/entry.txt`.
